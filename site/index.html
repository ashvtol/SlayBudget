It learns how you categorize

Change one transaction and the rest of that merchant follows, history included. A small model on your Mac learns from every choice and tells you why it picked a category. Cryptic statement lines like "TST* JOES" still land in the right place.

Every bill and subscription, handled

Recurring charges are found for you, with due dates and price changes. If a card pays a subscription back, you see each charge with its credit and what it really cost you. Renamed on your statement? It suggests merging.

Budgets you'll actually keep

Set a budget per category or group and see 24 months of spending against it, green when you're within it and orange when you're over. Roll leftovers over, rebalance in one click.

FIRE or soft saving, your call

Your money habits look across all your history: savings rate, your financial independence number, a suggested budget toward the goal you pick, and tips on the 50/30/20 split, subscriptions and no-spend days.

Drop in your statements

CSV, PDF, QFX, OFX, QuickBooks (QBO) or Quicken (QIF). It works out which account each statement belongs to, skips repeats, flags possible duplicates, and can watch a folder so new statements import themselves.

Questions

Is SlayBudget really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, with no paid tier and no ads. If it helps you, a star on GitHub helps other people find it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You download statements from your bank (CSV, PDF, QFX, OFX, QBO or QIF) and drop them in. Nothing asks for your bank password.

Where is my data stored?

In one SQLite file on your Mac. There's no server. Back it up like any other file, or with the one-click backup in Settings.

Is it a Copilot Money, YNAB or Mint alternative?

Yes, if you want budgets, categories, bills and insights without a subscription or giving an app your bank login. It's an independent project, not affiliated with any of them.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Windows and Linux builds are published too, but they're untested.

How do updates work?

SlayBudget checks GitHub for signed updates, shows what's new since your version, and installs only when you click.
